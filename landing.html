Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SiteScope and its architecture.

How does SiteScope scan websites without a backend server?
SiteScope is a modern static Progressive Web App. It communicates directly with public, high-availability APIs such as Google DNS-over-HTTPS (`dns.google`), Certificate Transparency logs (`crt.sh`), URLhaus malware feeds, and Lighthouse telemetry via standard client-side browser fetch requests and failover CORS proxy chains.
Is my search and domain audit history private?
Yes, completely. Because scans execute in your browser, your queries are never logged, tracked, or resold to data brokers. Scan history is saved locally in your browser's `localStorage`, with an optional encrypted cloud synchronization bucket.
What export formats are supported?
SiteScope exports diagnostic reports in structured JSON, GitHub Flavored Markdown (`.md`), printable high-resolution PDF layouts, as well as developer-focused **Postman Collections v2.1** and **OpenAPI 3.0 Specifications**.
How does the Uptime & SSL Expiry Watchlist work?
SiteScope stores your monitored domains in a persistent watchlist. When active, it performs periodic network health probes and Certificate Transparency queries, sending native browser Web Notifications if a site goes DOWN or if its SSL certificate is due to expire in less than 30 days.
